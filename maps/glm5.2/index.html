Language Explorer

Click any country on the map to see its official and widely spoken languages, each labelled with its language family. Use the search box to jump to a country, and toggle “Color by family” to paint the whole world by dominant language family.

Tips

  • Scroll / pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
  • Hover for the country name; click for details.
  • In family mode, click a legend entry to isolate that family.
  • Countries with embedded “official” status (e.g. Switzerland, Belgium, South Africa, India) show every official language.
